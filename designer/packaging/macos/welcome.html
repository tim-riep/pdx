pdx Designer @VERSION@

Designs PDF documents against an XML schema without writing XML. The result is PDF/A and PDF/UA files, checked with veraPDF.

The designer is installed only for you, into ~/Applications/pdx Designer.app, without administrator rights. Settings are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/pdx-designer.

To remove it, move the app from ~/Applications to the Trash.
